# Prospective order tax contract review

Local review only. This change follows the completed PR36 checkpoint on a separate branch and does not alter or integrate PR36.

## Contract and scope

New orders use `metadata.pricingContract = "tax-exclusive-v1"` alongside the existing shipping breakdown:

- `OrderItem.totalPrice` is the rounded quoted merchandise subtotal, excluding tax and shipping. Existing quoted discounts and adjustments are already included.
- `unitPrice` is a rounded display value. Do not multiply it back by quantity to reconstruct the authoritative line amount.
- `Order.subtotal` is the rounded sum of authoritative line amounts.
- `tax` and `shippingCost` are summed and rounded separately.
- `total = round2(subtotal + tax + shippingCost)`.

This matches the current cart's rounded-component contract. A pricing-engine quote total can be rounded from different intermediate values; it does not override this explicitly selected component contract.

Only new-order write-time assembly changes. The existing quote selection, configuration/design guards, shipping grouping, order lifecycle, notifications and consumer implementations are unchanged. No tax setting, material/rate row, quantity discount, vendor percentage, payment provider or checkout implementation changes.

The confirmed vendor-cost basis is product value before tax. PO grouping already consumes line totals, so new pre-tax lines supply that basis without modifying the PO service. Its existing 60% prototype formula is retained, not newly approved as a commercial vendor rate. A synthetic line previously gross110 now supplies net100 to grouping; the unchanged prototype formula would produce60 rather than66. No real PO or settlement was created or processed.

## History boundary

No database schema or historical migration is included. Order serialization does not reprice or reinterpret unmarked records. An isolated unmarked legacy sentinel with line110, subtotal110, tax10, shipping5 and total125 remained unchanged after new-order creation and read-back. This proves preservation of that fixture, not correctness of historical financial amounts.

Unmarked historical orders, including any pending downstream PO/payment operations, are not certified by this prospective correction. Their amounts and tax basis need separate review before real financial use; this patch does not infer past line taxes or recalculate existing POs/payments.
